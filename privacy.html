Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Summary

The Azan App shows prayer times, the Qibla direction, nearby mosques, the Quran and prayer reminders, and it collects no personal data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

To work out prayer times and find mosques near you, the app sends your location to the services listed below. Everything else, including your settings, prayer tracking and tasbih counts, stays on your device.

This policy applies to The Azan App, published by Murshid Musthafa ("we", "us").

Information the app uses

Only to provide its features. We never receive or store any of it ourselves.

  • Location (with your permission): Used for prayer times, the Qibla direction, your area name and nearby mosques. Your coordinates are sent to the services listed under Third-party services.
  • A city you choose: If you choose a city by hand, the name you type is sent to Apple's place search to find it, and the chosen place is saved on your device and used instead of your current location.
  • Compass heading: Used to point the Qibla arrow and the arrow toward the nearest mosque. Stays on your device.
  • Notifications (with your permission): Used for prayer, Tahajjud, Friday, Ramadan and Islamic day reminders, and the prayer countdown on the Lock Screen, all scheduled on your device.
  • Settings: Calculation method, Asr method, language, appearance, time format, reminder choices and sound. Stays on your device.
  • Tracking and reading: The prayers and fasts you tick, missed prayers, your Progress history, tasbih counts and your own dhikr, and where you stopped reading the Quran. Stays on your device.
  • Widgets: The coming week's prayer times and your ticks are shared with the app's own home screen and lock screen widgets, on your device only.
  • Saved prayer times: Kept so times still show when you are offline.

If you do not allow location access and do not choose a city, the app uses Dubai, UAE as its default location and sends those coordinates instead.

Third-party services

Each of these has its own privacy policy, which applies to the data it receives. None of them receives your name or any account details from the app.

  • Aladhan prayer-times API: Receives your latitude and longitude and the calculation and Asr methods, to return prayer times for your location, about once a week or when you change settings.
  • Apple location, place-name and Maps search: Your location, handled by iOS, to find your position, show your area name and find mosques near you; and the city name you type, if you search for one.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): Receives your location, to find mosques near you when the Nearest mosque card loads.
  • Apple WeatherKit: Receives your location, to show the current temperature and weather on Home and in the widgets. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.
  • Apple Maps directions: When you tap Directions, Apple Maps opens with the mosque's location; Apple Maps then works under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase: If you choose to support the app, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • App Store ratings: The app may show Apple's own rating prompt. Any rating or review goes to the App Store, not to us.

Apple's privacy policy

The Quran text, its English meaning and transliteration are built into the app, so reading them sends nothing.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or give your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Storage, deletion and your choices

Everything the app saves is stored only on your device, and deleting the app deletes all of it. We have no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

You can change permissions at any time in your phone's Settings, under The Azan App:

  • Location: turn it off and choose a city by tapping the location on Home, or the app uses Dubai, UAE.
  • Notifications: turn them off to stop all reminders.

In the app, you can switch each reminder on or off and choose its sound under Settings, then Prayer Notification.

Children, changes and contact

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

If a future version uses information differently, we will update this policy and its date before that version is released.
