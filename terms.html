Prayer times and Qibla direction are estimates

Prayer times are calculated from your location and a calculation method, chosen automatically for your country unless you pick one in Settings. Different methods, local conventions, elevation and location accuracy can shift times by several minutes. The Qibla direction depends on your device's compass, which can be affected by magnets, metal objects and calibration.

Please use the app as a helpful guide, not as a religious authority. When it matters, confirm times and direction with your local mosque or a trusted scholar. Reminders depend on your notification settings and may be delayed or silenced by Focus, Low Power Mode or other device settings.
